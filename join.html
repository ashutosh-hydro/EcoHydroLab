Join Us

Work with us

We are always interested in hearing from motivated students and researchers who want to work at the intersection of hydrology, ecology, machine learning and climate.

Who we're looking for

Strong candidates typically have a background in civil/environmental engineering, hydrology, earth sciences, physics, or a quantitative discipline, along with:

  • Programming experience (Python preferred) and comfort with data
  • Interest in modeling, machine learning or field/remote-sensing methods
  • Curiosity and persistence for open research problems

Current openings

Update this section as positions open and close.

PhD: Prospective students should apply through the IIT Roorkee admissions process. Reach out beforehand describing your interests.

Postdoc / Project staff: Positions are advertised when funded — email to express interest.

Interns / MTech projects: IIT Roorkee students are welcome to enquire about projects.

How to apply

Get in touch

Email the PI with your CV, a short statement of research interests, and a note on why EcoHydroLab specifically. Mention any relevant coursework, projects or publications.
