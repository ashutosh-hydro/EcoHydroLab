What we do

Research areas

We work across scales — from plot-level ecohydrology to continental large-sample datasets — bridging process understanding and data-driven prediction.

Large-sample hydrology

Building and using continental catchment datasets to learn general hydrological behaviour across hundreds of basins.

Process-based & data-driven modeling

Physics-informed and deep learning models (LSTM, Transformers, differentiable hybrids) for streamflow and beyond.

Climate change & extremes

Compound hydro-climatic extremes, flood frequency, and impacts under a changing climate.

Terrestrial ecohydrology

Vegetation–water–carbon interactions, ecohydrological indicators and ecosystem response.

Ecosystem services

Quantifying and mapping ecosystem services, with a focus on the Himalayas.

Water accounting & the nexus

Water accounting frameworks and the water–energy–food nexus for resource management.

Flagship dataset

CAMELS-IND

Catchment Attributes and Meteorology for Large-sample Studies – India. Daily meteorological time series, observed and LSTM-predicted streamflow, and static catchment attributes — built to bring Indian catchments into global hydrological research.
